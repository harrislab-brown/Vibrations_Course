## Custom Mount

The custom mount is printed vertically with supports
0.2mm layer height
5 perimeters 

After printing, supports are removed the holes to secure the nut and plastic rod are tapped 8-32. 
The two holes on the large flat face for securing a VibeCheck sensor board do not need to be tapped. 
Use the M3 self tapping screws for plastic along with the T10 Torx driver to attach the sensor circuit board to the 3D print.
